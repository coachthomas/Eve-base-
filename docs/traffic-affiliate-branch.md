# Energy Culture: traffic, discovery and affiliate revenue branch

October 6, 2026. Research-backed proposal for review; no program enrollment or Wix publication performed. Commission rates are advertised current terms, not guaranteed earnings or acceptance. Merchant contracts govern actual eligibility, attribution, reversals and payouts.

## Architecture decision

Incorporate this as a connected branch of Energy Culture, with a dedicated visitor-facing resource hub and separate revenue measurements. Share the offer catalog, editorial calendar and aggregate reporting with lead discovery. Keep customer coaching information outside affiliate targeting.

Proposed navigation: **Coaching · Books & Resources · Watch & Learn · Stories & Podcast · About**. Put Useful Finds inside Books & Resources initially. Preserve the site's established fonts, colors, spacing, buttons and mobile layout when implementation begins; this proposal has not inspected current live styling.

The commercial path is: useful search/social content → relevant hub page → book/product purchase, newsletter signup or coaching exploration → repeat visit. Visitors choose their route. Affiliate buyers are not automatically coaching leads, and an affiliate purchase does not prove a $150/$400 coaching budget.

Breadth is welcome, but expansion should earn its place through demand, useful editorial coverage and positive economics. A coherent hub can have something for many interests without forcing every visitor through a generic product wall.

## What the hub should contain

### 1. Books & Resources — launch anchor

Organize shelves by what a reader wants: Find Your Direction; Live More Authentically; Habits & Everyday Growth; Spirituality & Wisdom; Relationships & Communication; Curious Minds; Stories & Family Reading. Thomas's STAR books get a distinct author shelf, with verified current purchase links.

Each selection has an original short description, who it may suit, a limitation/alternative, format and edition, and clear external buying options. Include new and used options where available. Compare total delivered price, condition and edition, not just a headline price. Do not claim every used copy has the same condition or guarantee inventory.

Start without holding inventory: the retailer takes payment and handles fulfillment/returns. Directly reselling used books later is a separate model requiring stock, condition descriptions, shipping and returns economics.

Potential seed authors/topics for Thomas to review include Ernest Holmes and the Four Agreements tradition already discussed, plus research candidates on habits, meaning, communication and self-reflection. Do not label a book personally read or a guru a favorite without Thomas's confirmation.

### 2. Watch & Learn

Curate official YouTube channels/videos and eligible embedded playlists with original commentary: what the lesson covers, why it is useful, and a related reading or coaching path. Candidate themes: Science of Mind, spirituality, relationships, habits, consciousness, creativity and thoughtful human–AI collaboration.

Use YouTube's official embed when enabled or link to the original; retain creator credit and player controls. Do not download/rehost videos, imply a creator partnership, or publish their full transcripts. Embedding a creator's video does not itself pay Energy Culture; related approved affiliate links, sponsorships or coaching bookings provide revenue. A lightweight thumbnail/link or click-to-load embed can keep mobile pages manageable.

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780

### 3. Useful Finds — controlled broader exploration

Test journals/planners, reading lights/book stands, home-office accessories, creative tools, practical gifts, gardening, outdoor and motorcycle-related goods. These are candidate categories, not proven sellers on Energy Culture. Give each a real reason to exist and a well-researched guide. Higher commission alone is insufficient; quality, fit, delivered cost, support and conversion all matter.

Broader experiments can attract people who later discover coaching. Treat that as a measurable hypothesis, not guaranteed cross-selling. Initial editorial allocation proposal: 60% books/self-help/coaching-adjacent, 25% related interests, 15% broader experiments. Change it using actual results.

### 4. Reasons to return

Reading theme of the month; one useful video selection; new original article; podcast/guest notes; refreshed budget book options; visitor-requested resource topics; a concise opt-in email digest. New/updated useful content earns return visits better than static affiliate banners.

Optional later expansions: online book discussions, creator interviews, licensed digital resources, carefully labeled sponsorships and local event calendars. These add workload, moderation or agreements, so stage them after the first branch performs.

## Traffic strategy and measurement

1. **Search:** original topic pages answering specific questions, such as "new versus used self-help books: what to compare" or a carefully researched reading guide about authenticity. Include useful comparisons, evidence, alternatives and transparent authorship; do not pretend to have personally tested products. Google identifies copied merchant descriptions without added value as thin affiliation.
2. **Pinterest:** visual reading lists, gift/resource guides and original graphics that link to the matching site page. Pinterest Trends can inform demand and seasonality; trends are not purchase guarantees.
3. **YouTube/social:** Thomas's short original observations or podcast excerpts with a clear link to the relevant reading/watch page. Respect community posting rules; no unsolicited link flooding.
4. **Opt-in email:** digest links back to Energy Culture, with preferences and easy unsubscribe. Apply each merchant's channel rules to direct affiliate links; do not assume all channels are allowed.
5. **Partnerships:** reading groups, independent bookstores, podcasts and relevant community hosts; prepare useful co-created resources and introductions for Thomas to approve/send.
6. **Paid advertising:** only after validated unit economics and merchant permission. Some programs limit brand bidding, coupons, direct linking and promotional methods. No spending is authorized by this plan.

Demand tools: Google Trends, Pinterest Trends, Search Console query/impression/click reports, and existing site analytics. Combine search interest, relevant user questions, merchant bestseller/trend evidence obtained through permitted tools, competition and expected margin. A trend or retailer ranking does not establish Energy Culture conversion performance.

Measure by category, page and campaign: visits, repeat visits, affiliate clicks, network-confirmed transactions, approved commissions, reversals, email opt-ins, coaching-page visits, inquiries, bookings and collected coaching revenue. Affiliate clicks are not sales. Attribute later coaching only where supported by consented tracking or the client's self-report; merchant buyer identities usually will not be available. Keep affiliate commissions and coaching revenue separate to avoid double counting.

Illustrative arithmetic, not a forecast: a qualifying $20 book at 10% yields about $2 before adjustments. One $150 coaching purchase equals 75 such gross commissions; one $400 purchase equals 200. If affiliate revenue averages $0.04 per visitor, a $0.20-per-visitor campaign loses money before any demonstrable coaching contribution. Books can earn directly while developing attention and trust, but ad economics must be tested.

## Branch parameters and functions

Keep separate `content_interest`, `affiliate_intent` and `coaching_intent` fields. Someone browsing a guru video can remain anonymous and enjoy it without becoming a sales prospect. The prior three paid lead tiers still require separate coaching-fit and price-specific payment evidence.

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `register_affiliate_program` | Terms, approval state, merchant/network, geography, rates, attribution, permitted channels, asset rights and review date |
| `research_demand` | Topic/category evidence, source/date, seasonality, competition and uncertainty |
| `score_resource_candidate` | Editorial relevance, reader value, evidence quality, economic potential and maintenance burden; explain component scores |
| `verify_resource` | Edition/condition/availability, claims, source evidence, image/embed rights and update needs |
| `build_resource_page` | Original editorial content, approved external links/disclosures, relevant internal next steps and existing site design |
| `select_related_offer` | Topic-based coaching suggestion, presented as optional; no diagnosis or affordability inference |
| `validate_affiliate_link` | Approved program ID, merchant destination, allowed channel, tracking format and no hidden misleading redirects |
| `refresh_catalog` | Expired products, unavailable used listings, broken embeds, rates and old content flagged for review |
| `record_campaign` | Source/medium/content tags and consent-aware aggregate events |
| `reconcile_commissions` | Network reports → pending/approved/reversed/paid; never infer earnings from clicks |
| `evaluate_branch` | Contribution, workload, return visits and evidenced assisted coaching conversions by category |

Minimum resource fields: ID, topic, merchant, affiliate approval, destination/link ID, ISBN/edition where applicable, condition, format, editorial rationale, evidence URLs, availability date, image/content rights, commission terms, disclosure, related page/offer, status, last review.

Program approval and content value are mandatory gates. Use a provisional scoring model for relevance/value/economics/maintenance after those gates; scores are editorial priorities, not conversion probabilities. Queue low-confidence or broad categories for controlled tests.

## Trust and practical rules

Place a clear disclosure near recommendations: **"Energy Culture may earn a commission if you buy through these links."** Follow each program's required additional wording, including Amazon's prescribed Associate identification if used. FTC guidance requires clear, conspicuous disclosure; a footer alone is not a reliable substitute near endorsements.

https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

Use authorized merchant assets/feeds or original/licensed imagery; a product listing is not automatic image permission. Follow sponsored-link search guidance when implemented. Describe opinions as opinions and support factual product/benefit claims. Disclose sponsorship, gifted items and personal testing status accurately.
